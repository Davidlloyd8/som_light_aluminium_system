Types of Aluminium Roofing Sheets

We offer a variety of aluminium roofing sheets to suit different architectural styles and functional requirements. Our range includes options that provide durability, weather resistance, and aesthetic appeal for residential, commercial, and industrial applications.

Longspan Corrugation Metra

A durable and versatile aluminium roofing sheet with a long-span design, ideal for large areas and industrial applications.

Longspan Normal Corrugation

A lightweight and cost-effective aluminium roofing sheet with a standard corrugated profile, suitable for residential and commercial projects.

Metcopo

A high-quality aluminium roofing sheet with a sleek and modern design, providing excellent weather resistance and durability for various applications.

Step Tiles

A stylish aluminium roofing sheet with a stepped tile design, offering a unique aesthetic and reliable performance for residential and commercial buildings.
